What is S.L.O.T. Machine?

A workstation that thinks like a drummer, not a sequencer.

S.L.O.T. Machine (Synchronized Loop Orchestration & Timing) is an intuative, performance-ready, polyrhythmic drum sequencer. Load up to 16 samples, dial in the Count, Beat-Mask and Decay for each one, and let the engine handle rock-solid timing while you focus on the groove.

16 slots, one brain.

Every slot has its own Count, Beat-Mask, Volume, Decay, mute/solo, and MIDI channel. Need straight 4-on-the-floor kicks with triplet hats and a snare that lands every 3rd bar? Go for it. S.L.O.T. Machine keeps everything locked to the master tempo so your weird ideas still hit tight.

  • Count-driven timing. Choose how many hits per loop instead of guessing note values.
  • Beat-Masking. Turn individual hits on/off. In an 8 beat loop, you could turn off beats 5 and 7, completely changing the feel of the loop. This is a game changer.
  • Per-slot Decay. Tighten kicks, open hats, or create gated percussion without touching a compressor.
  • Embedded drum kit. Get started instantly with built-in kicks, snares, hats and more, or drag in your own samples.
  • Per-slot MIDI channels. Trigger external instruments, layer hardware, or route drums to separate DAW tracks.
From patterns to full songs.

You have unlimited Tabs - each Tab holds it's own 16-slot pattern. With Playthrough mode, S.L.O.T. Machine will walk down the tabs, repeat each one as many times as you choose, and optionally loop the entire sequence. It’s like having an arrangement view built straight into your drum machine.
